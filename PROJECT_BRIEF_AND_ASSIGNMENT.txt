PROJECT 01 — RETAIL SALES & PROFITABILITY ANALYSIS
Synthetic practice dataset | India | 2025 | 10,018 rows supplied (10,000 generated rows + 18 duplicate records)

BUSINESS SCENARIO
You are a Junior Data Analyst supporting a multi-category retailer. Management wants to understand sales, profitability, product performance, regional performance and customer behaviour.

BUSINESS QUESTIONS
1. What are total net sales, recorded cost and recorded profit?
2. Which categories and products contribute most to sales and profit?
3. Which regions and cities perform best and worst?
4. How do sales and profit trend month by month?
5. Which channels and customer segments contribute most?
6. Which products have high sales but weak profit margins?
7. What data-quality issues could affect management reporting?
8. What actions would you recommend?

DATA QUALITY ISSUES INTENTIONALLY INCLUDED
- 18 exact duplicate rows.
- Missing values in selected fields.
- Inconsistent text case and extra spaces.
- Some invalid city labels.
- Zero and negative quantities.
- Discount percentages above 100%.
- A few dates stored as text.
- Returned orders have negative sales and profit; cancelled orders have zero sales and profit.
This is synthetic data, not actual company data. Do not assume every negative value is an error. Investigate business meaning before changing anything.

   Issue_ID | Column_Name | Observed_Issue | Possible_Business_Impact | Proposed_Action
